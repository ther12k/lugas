## Contamination assessment

No competing soak/build/test workload returned during any phase. Ambient
5-minute load drifted 3.0 → ~4.5 across the ~20-minute session (desktop
background); 1-minute peaks during tsc/benchmark phases are the
measurements' own multi-core processes. Every result sits far enough from
its threshold (≥21% typecheck headroom; ≥29% rps headroom above targets)
that this drift cannot change any classification. No sample is flagged
contaminated; the drift is recorded rather than hidden.
