### Which rule the qualification is made under (owner-review reconciliation, 2026-09-15)

The repository contains three layers of quiet-host wording; the closure
record names the one applied and its basis:

1. **Governing policy** — `docs/performance-gates.md`: evidence captured on
   an idle machine; no concurrent builds, tests, soak jobs, or other
   significant load; loaded-machine failures are environmental. *Held:* no
   soak, no builds, no external test execution (the only test run was the
   gate's own suite).
2. **Attestation procedure rule** — `docs/reports/gates/M6.md` (#309, the
   settled M6R6 procedure that this verification executes): *quiet host,
   sustained load < 4*. *Held:* all three windows at qualification were
   below 4 (2.96 / 2.71 / 2.97), and the benchmark-phase ambient recorded
   into the evidence artifact was 3.96/3.02/3.06 before and 3.54/3.02/3.06
   after. The accepted M8/beta.4 attestation precedent on this same host
   class ran at 2.73/3.29/2.91 before and 3.61/3.43/2.98 after.
3. **CA-12's operational heuristic** — `docs/reports/issues/CA-12.md`
   records, for CA-12's own planned narrow typecheck check, an
   "operational" restatement: 1-min < 2.0 AND 15-min < 3.0, and no process
   > 30% CPU. **This run did not meet that stricter pair** (1-min 2.96;
   the executing session's own runtime exceeded 30% CPU). Disclosed, not
   waived: ODR-0020 — the later owner decision that defines THIS
   verification — requires the *documented quiet-host conditions* (layer 1)
   re-verified ahead of the full M6R6 attestation (layer 2); CA-12's pair
   governs its own narrower planned measurement, is stricter than every
   accepted attestation in the repository's history (M8's accepted
   before-load 1-min was 2.73), and its >30%-CPU clause is unmeetable
   during any agent-executed verification because the executing session's
   own runtime exceeds it. Final endorsement that layer 2 governs this
   closure rests with the owner.

## Contamination assessment

All recorded gate measurements passed their unchanged thresholds. Host
observations recorded no identified competing workload during the
measurement phases. The five-minute load increased during execution
(ambient 3.0 → ~4.5 across the session); its causal contribution to
individual measurements was **not quantified** — load average counts
runnable/uninterruptible tasks over time (including the measurement's own
multi-core processes: TypeScript 7's compiler, the benchmark servers, and
the verify gate's own build/test phases); it is neither a percentage
slowdown nor a bounded measurement-error estimate. A defensible reading
is therefore only this: every recorded result passed despite the recorded
load history. No sample is flagged contaminated (no competing workload
was identified); the drift is recorded rather than hidden, and no
counterfactual claim is made about what the numbers would have been on a
fully idle host.
