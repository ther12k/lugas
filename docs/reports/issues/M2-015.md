## Outcome

Completed. Documented and verified the architectural boundary between Bun and Lugas regarding body limits and native stream pass-through. Server-level body constraints are delegated to Bun's native `maxRequestBodySize` option, while routes without a body schema leave `request.body` (ReadableStream), `request.text()`, `request.formData()`, and `request.arrayBuffer()` completely untouched with zero pre-reading.

## Security considerations

- Lugas does not attempt to buffer request streams beyond Bun's configured limits.
- Handlers on routes without declared body schemas receive unconsumed streams directly from the runtime.
- Malformed or oversize errors never reflect raw request body slices in response details.
