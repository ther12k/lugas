## Assumptions

- Margin method: releaseBlock = 0.85x median, alert = 0.92x median, target = median rounded to nearest 100; typecheck budget = median + ~15% headroom (v2 precedent x1.14). Margins are sized to observed same-host variance, not to v2 absolute values. Known cosmetic consequence: an upward rounding can put target just above the measured median (the 17-rps plain-static alert); a future derivation may round targets down.
- Single-session derivation (one quiet window). Cross-session drift on this shared host is real (comparator column above); a second derivation session to tighten margins is owner-optional, not assumed.
- Absolute RPS is host-relative; v3 numbers are NOT comparable to v2 laptop numbers.
- Benchmarks measure the co-tenant slice as-is; craneocr production traffic during measurement is part of the accepted environment.

- SECURITY-RELEVANT FINDING for owner review (pre-existing, not introduced here): the assets.dirs symlink-containment guarantee (ADR-0018 amendment) holds only where openat2 exists (Linux >= 5.0). On kernel 4.15 + Bun 1.4.0 the outside-root symlink IS served — fail-open.
